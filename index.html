How mod loading works

LOCLM gives your DLL access to the game data before launch.

01

LOCLM reads the original data.win.

02

It scans loclm/mods/ for mod folders.

03

It reads modinfo.json and resolves load order.

04

It loads your DLL and calls Load(int audiogroup, UndertaleData data).

05

Your mod patches resources in memory.

06

LOCLM writes the patched cache and starts the game.
